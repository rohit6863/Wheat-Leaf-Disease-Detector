Diseases Detected

Fusarium Head Blight

Also known as scab, causes bleached spikelets and shriveled grains. Major threat to wheat yield.

Healthy Wheat

No visible disease; vibrant green leaves with no deformities, spots, or discoloration.

Leaf Rust

Caused by Puccinia triticina, appears as small orange-brown pustules on leaf surfaces.

Tan Spot

Fungal disease causing tan to brown spots with yellow halos on wheat leaves.

About the Wheat Leaf Disease Detector

This tool helps farmers and researchers identify wheat leaf diseases using an AI model trained with ResNet50. Just upload a photo to detect issues like Fusarium Head Blight, Leaf Rust, Tan Spot, or confirm a healthy wheat leaf. The model provides high accuracy predictions to help with early disease detection and crop management.
